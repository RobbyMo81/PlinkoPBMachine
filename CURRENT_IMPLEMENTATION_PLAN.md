Design of provisional WB construction: uniform random sample over the finite set of distinct five-white-ball combinations with exact required sum, conditional on at most two shared whites with each already selected experimental ticket. Bounded attempts (e.g. 100,000 per ticket) fail with structured constraint error, never alter selected sum/PB or overlap rule. Sampling conditional on prior ticket ordering is not a claim of independent white sets, only unbiased feasible selection. Test with small enumerated pools and deterministic RNG injection; document finite retry failure probability. Control uses separate SystemRandom draws of five unique whites and PB, no experimental filters; if it duplicates one of nine, record collision, do not redraw.

Portfolio: ranks 1–3 sums and within each the top 3 observed PBs; nine assigned pairs in order. Preserve nested observations/metadata, all 36 overlap checks, source hash, snapshot dataset hash, target draw date and generated-at timestamp.
